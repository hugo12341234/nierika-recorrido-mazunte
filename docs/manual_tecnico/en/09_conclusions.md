# 9. Conclusions

> **Status (Nov 12, 2026): honest draft.** Only what is already true is written. Final conclusions (FPS, errors, tests on another PC, videos) are completed after E-1/E-3; **nothing is claimed without measuring**.

## 9.1 What was achieved against the acceptance criteria

See the table in [§1.4](01_introduction.md). In short, to date:

- **Complete scene in code:** beach and sea with waves, cemetery with 4 tombs, cross, rock, wall and papel picado, and the room with a 3-level altar and offering ✅.
- **5 animations** implemented (2 by KeyFrames, 3 simple) with mathematical justification and code values ([§7](07_math_justification.md)).
- **23 models with `.obj` and `.blend`** in the repository; the target is ≥ 18 using the Oct 19 definition of "own" ([§6](06_models_and_resources.md)).
- **Collisions, 5 cards, own audio, Blinn-Phong lighting, `cleanup()` and an FPS counter** in the code.

**What no document can state yet:** that 30 FPS is met (not measured), that the program runs on another PC without Visual Studio (E-3 untested), that the audio sounds right, or that everything marked 🔄 builds without errors in MSVC.

## 9.4 Lessons learned (proposal; D1 and D2 confirm or change) ❓

These lessons come from facts recorded in the repository, not opinion:

1. **A quick review does not catch everything:** the wall (B-10) had `/verificar` APPROVE and was seen compiled on D2's PC, yet it was **mirrored in z** until E-2 found it on Nov 12. PRs #1–#7 and #59–#68 also entered without another person's verdict.
2. **Inheriting the base project with its errors:** the received solution was 32-bit with scales off 1 unit = 1 m (Oct 19 decisions and A-2).
3. **An environment without a Windows compiler forces testing outside OpenGL:** the math was checked with separate programs (23 checks in D-7), but that **does not replace** seeing the scene.
4. **Define values early:** several values (collisions, lights, timings) were not in the documents and were proposed on the fly.
5. **The calendar was compressed:** many closures concentrated between Nov 3 and Nov 12.
